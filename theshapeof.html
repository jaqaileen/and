ABOUT
The Shape of Water

This analysis essay turned artist book explores the duality of women in Greek mythology through the lens of water: a force that both nurtures life and brings destruction. This project reimagines classical myths to examine how femininity is often tied to nature’s most fluid and unpredictable element.

Size: 8 x 11in, handmade resin cover
Binding: Coptic Stitch
Typeface: Carta Nueva Display, Faculty Glyphic, Big Caslon CC
