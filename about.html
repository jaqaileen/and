ABOUT

Jaq Aileen is an artist, graphic designer, and storyteller. Her practice spans print, digital, and spatial mediums, with a focus on materiality. Working with print, light, projection, textiles, she explores composition, scale, and depth to invite movement and interaction.


She has a BFA in Communication Design and a minor in Creative Entrepreneurship from Parsons School of Design.
