ABOUT

Bodies of Water explores the concept of hydrofeminism which proposes that the human body is not a closed or isolated entity, but a porous body of water in constant exchange with the world around it. Through this lens, women’s bodies can be understood not as contained forms, but as aqueous and relational—continuously interacting with, shaped by, and ultimately returning to water.

THE PROCESS

This artist book translates key ideas from hydrofeminism into an accessible, visual, and textual format, framing the body as materially entangled with global water systems. The wooden cubes, book, and container, emphasize the body’s fluidity and permeability, reinforcing the idea that the human body is composed of and sustained by water. The tactile, sculptural format invites physical engagement with these intimate and often stigmatized substances.
