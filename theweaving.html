ABOUT
THE WEAVING
This interactive card game celebrates the art of connection through 80 cards across 10 themes. Players weave their ideas together by physically interlocking the cards through slits, building layered structures that grow into intricate forms — both visually and conceptually. The cards are kept in a handmade rattan weaved box with button and tie closure.

SIZE: 3 x 4.5 x 2.5 in
TYPEFACE: Geographica Script, Geographica Regular
TYPE
Card Game
YEAR

Inspired by the Eames playing cards, these playing cards are also designed to interlock when making connections.
